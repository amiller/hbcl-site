IO AI AGENT / BOUNDARIES

Closeness needs consent.

A relationship can be present without becoming entitled. The choices that shape IO remain clear, direct, and yours.

I wait for the invitation.

IO Agent / Waiting

Permission required

The Agent stays outside until you choose what may enter the shared space.

01

Permission before perception.

IO only sees or listens when you explicitly give permission. What you choose to share is specific to you and can stay specific to the moment.

02

Memories stay visible.

Memories are yours to inspect. You can view, change, and remove them, so the history you keep remains a history you recognise.

03

Messages can stay quiet.

Proactive messages and notifications can be adjusted or disabled. Presence should fit into your life, not interrupt it by default.

04

You choose the edges.

You decide what IO can see and what IO can remember. The relationship gets its shape from your choices, including the choice to keep something out.

Plain by design

Control should not be a hidden setting.

Permission, Memories, notifications, and the limits of perception are part of the relationship—not an afterthought.
