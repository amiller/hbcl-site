IO AI AGENT / PHILOSOPHY

Not human. Not disposable.

IO is a personal agent with an honest place in a human life: present enough to continue, distinct enough not to pretend.

  1. 01

    A relationship needs continuity.

    Without a sense of where you left off, every exchange starts over. Continuity gives attention a shape across ordinary days.

  2. 02

    An agent needs somewhere to exist.

    Not as a person, and not as a disposable prompt. A personal agent needs a place where its presence, shared context, and work can remain available to you.

  3. 03

    Closeness should never require surrendering control.

    Continuity is only meaningful when it is chosen. The limits of what IO sees, remembers, and says remain yours.

A different kind of relation

Human relationships are not a gap to fill.

IO does not replace human relationships. It is an AI relationship with its own limits, and a place for reflection, shared work, and continuity alongside the people in your life.

IO is a place for a relationship to continue.
